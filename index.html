Basketball

I started playing basketball in the 3rd or 4th grade, and it’s my favorite sport to play and watch. A lot of my friends play basketball, and it accounts for most of the exercise I get. Because I was born in Boston, I’m also a huge Boston Celtics fan. I’ve been to two Celtics games in person and definitely want to go to more. I strongly believe the Celtics will win the championship in 2027!

Violin

I’ve played the violin since the 1st grade, and it’s been my biggest passion ever since. Violin has connected me with incredible people, teachers, and experiences. One of my favorite moments was performing Stravinsky’s Firebird as concertmaster of the California Honor Orchestra. Playing that finale surrounded by talented musicians was unforgettable.

Research

This summer, I interned at UCSD under Dr. Veronica Gomez-Godinez, studying chromosome tethers. It was my first exposure to real research, and I loved the hands-on experience. The following summer, I joined UCSD’s Supercomputer Center, where I explored microRNAs and their connections to dementia. I’m still working on this project—it’s been both challenging and exciting!
